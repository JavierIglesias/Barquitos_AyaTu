Privacy policy

Barquitos AyaTu · com.ayatu.barquitos

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Versión en español

In one line: Barquitos AyaTu does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything it saves stays on your phone, and the app has no servers.

Who is responsible

Barquitos AyaTu is a free, personal app written by Javier Iglesias. There is no company behind it, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

What is stored, and where

The app saves a few things in the phone's own private storage, which no other app can read. None of it leaves the device.

WhatWhy
Your settings (sound, vibration, animations, theme, rules)To remember how you like to play
Your statistics (games, wins, shots, medals)To show your history on the Statistics screen
The game in progressSo you can carry on later
The name you choose to play underSo the other player can see your name in a two-player game
An activity log (date, time and what happened)To diagnose problems; you can read and delete it from inside the app

You can erase all of it at any time by uninstalling the app, or from phone Settings → Apps → Barquitos AyaTu → Storage → Clear data.

What is sent

Nothing to the internet. The app has no servers, uses no analytics services, carries no advertising and contains no third-party libraries that send information anywhere.

The only communication that exists is the Two players (on the same Wi-Fi) mode: the two phones find each other on the local network and talk directly to each other, without going through any server. Only what the game needs travels over that connection: the name you chose, the rules of the game, where your ships are and your shots. None of it is stored anywhere once the game is over.

Permissions, and what they are for

PermissionWhy
INTERNETTo open the direct connection to the other phone on the local network. No internet server is contacted.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo know whether Wi-Fi is available before looking for games, so it can tell you if it is not.
VIBRATEA short buzz on a hit and a long one when a ship sinks. It can be switched off in Settings.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, shared storage or advertising identifiers.

Android backup

The app allows Android's automatic backup. If you have it enabled on your phone, Android may copy the settings and statistics to your own Google Drive account, under Google's terms. That is handled by the operating system, not by the app, and you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone. Since no data is collected at all, no data is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated and the new date shown at the top.
